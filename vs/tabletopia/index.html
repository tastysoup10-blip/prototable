Questions

Is Prototable a good alternative to Tabletopia?

Yes, if you are still designing it: making cards from templates, changing them between and during sessions, and collecting what players think. Prototable has all 11 of the capabilities we compare; Tabletopia has 2.

When is Tabletopia the better choice?

When your game is finished and you want it in front of a big audience of players, or you are a publisher planning a commercial digital edition. It is stronger at: a large audience of players and a big catalog; apps for Steam, iOS and Android; video chat in the game; a path for publishers to sell digital editions.

How does Tabletopia's price compare with Prototable's?

Tabletopia: For designers: Basic is free for 1 game; Indie is $14.99 and Publisher $49.97. Prototable: Free for two games, with 2 players a room. Designer is $59 a year; Studio $99 a year.

Can I move my game from Tabletopia to Prototable?

Prototable imports a spreadsheet or CSV, a folder of images, PDFs made for print, or a Tabletop Simulator card sheet cut into cards. Players join free from a link.
