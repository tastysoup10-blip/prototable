Prototable: The digital kitchen table for tabletop prototypes

Design your cards, playtest them with friends in the browser, and change them mid‑game.

Create your own game. Free. No account, nothing to install. Your game stays in the browser until you sign up to keep it.

What you can do

Seamless multiplayer: Send a link. Everyone is at the table.

Friends join from a link in their browser and every move lands for everyone as it happens. No installs, no accounts, and players are always free.

  • Moves, draws and rolls show up for everyone at once
  • Hands, seats and turns for each player
  • Chat, pings and live cursors
  • A PIN or your approval to join, if you like

Import from anywhere: Bring the game you already have.

Nobody wants to retype a deck. A spreadsheet, a folder of images, a PDF or a Tabletop Simulator sheet becomes cards on the table in a minute.

  • Tabletop Simulator card sheets, cut into cards
  • Spreadsheets and CSV, one row per card
  • A folder of images
  • PDFs made for print, a card a page
  • Read the fields off a card sheet (Designer)

Rapid iteration: Change the game mid‑game.

Edit a card while everyone is still playing and the new version lands on the table at once. Record the session, then keep or discard every change.

  • Every edit reaches every copy: table, hands and decks
  • Notes on cards and feedback from players, saved to the session
  • Before and after for each change, kept or discarded in Review
  • Restore any session to play on from there

Templates: Lay out a card once. Every card follows.

Design the frame, the fields and the back. Change the template and the whole deck updates.

  • Your own fields, fonts, colors and icons
  • Card backs, borders and landscape cards
  • Token templates, from discs to hexes
  • Edit any card while it is on the table

Macros and automations: One button runs the whole deal.

Snap steps together into a button, or let the table run a rule for you. No code.

  • "Shuffle, then deal 5 to everyone" on one click
  • Type "deal 3" in the command box and it happens
  • Automations: reshuffle when the deck runs out, and more
  • Macros that ask how many before they run

A sandbox for any game: If it goes on a table, it goes on this one.

Cards, boards, tokens, dice, chess pieces, grids and lanes on a 3D table that feels real, so whatever your game needs is already here.

  • Physics dice from d4 to d20, and foil finishes
  • Square and hex grids, lanes, mats and piles
  • Tokens and counters you can clip to cards
  • Stand-ins to play every seat yourself

Questions

What is Prototable?

A browser tool for designing and playtesting tabletop game prototypes: build your cards and pieces, play them with other people on a shared 3D table from a link, and change the game between (or during) playtests.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on a laptop, desktop, iPad or phone.

Do my players need an account or to pay?

No. Players you invite join free, from a link. You can start without an account too, and sign up when you want to keep your game.

Can I bring in cards I already made?

Yes: from a spreadsheet or CSV, a folder of card images, PDFs made for print, or a card sheet such as a Tabletop Simulator deck. Or design cards from templates inside Prototable.

Can I change cards during a playtest?

Yes. Edit a card and everyone at the table sees the new version right away, without restarting the game.

How does it help me learn from a playtest?

Record a session and Prototable keeps the notes left on cards, what each card did, and feedback from players. Review lists every change to keep or discard, and on Designer how each card is playing.

Can I print my game?

Yes. Print sheets with cut guides for a paper playtest. Paid plans print without a watermark.
