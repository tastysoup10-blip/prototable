How to play Checkers

The goal: Take all twelve of your opponent’s pieces, or leave them with no move.

Setup

Each side has 12 pieces on the dark squares nearest them. Dark moves first, then you take turns. Press Next on the bar after your move.

Moving

  • Move one piece diagonally forward one square, onto an empty dark square.
  • Jump to capture: hop diagonally over an enemy piece into the empty square beyond, and take it off the board. Step your side's has taken bar up by one.
  • If a jump is open to you, you must take it. After a jump, keep jumping with the same piece if you can.

Kings

Reach the far row and your piece is crowned: drop one of your captured pieces on top of it and they stack into a king. A king moves and jumps diagonally forward or backward.

Winning

Take all twelve of your opponent's pieces (the table calls it when a bar fills), or block them so they can't move.

To start over with a fresh table, leave the room and press Play again.
