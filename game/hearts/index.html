How to play Hearts

The goal: Finish with the lowest score. Every heart you take costs a point, and the Queen of Spades costs 13.

Setup

Hearts is for four players. Press Deal for 4 and everyone gets 13 cards.

Three of you? Press Deal for 3. Everyone gets 17 cards, and one card is left over. Whoever wins the first trick takes it, points and all. If the 2 of Clubs is the card left over, the lowest club leads instead.

Passing

Before you play, choose three cards from your hand and drag them onto the Passed pile of the player you're passing to. The table says which way this hand goes:

  1. Hand 1: to your left
  2. Hand 2: to your right
  3. Hand 3: across
  4. Hand 4: no passing

Once everyone has passed, press Pick up passed, and three cards land in each hand. With three players there's nobody across: don't pass on hands 3 and 4.

Tricks

Playing a trick

Whoever holds the 2 of Clubs plays it onto the Trick row first. Then go clockwise, one card each.

  • Follow suit if you can. If you can't, play anything.
  • The highest card of the suit that was led wins. Aces are high, and nothing is trump.
  • Nobody plays a heart or the Queen of Spades on the first trick, unless that's all they hold.
  • You can't lead a heart until one has been played on an earlier trick, unless you hold nothing else. The table says when hearts are broken.

The winner presses Take trick: the cards go on their Tricks pile, and they lead next.

Scoring

The table adds up your Score as you take tricks:

  • Each heart: 1 point
  • Queen of Spades: 13 points

Shooting the moon: take all 26 points in one hand, and everyone else takes the 26 instead. Change the scores by hand: 26 off yours, 26 on each of theirs.

Deal again for the next hand. The game ends when someone reaches 100, and the lowest score wins. New game puts every score back to zero.
