FOOD YOUR WAY OUT!

OFFICIAL GAME RULEBOOK

Eat smart. Trust nobody. Survive.

1. GAME OVERVIEW

Food Your Way Out! is a fast-paced survival card game where players must avoid poisonous food, use special action cards strategically, and outlast their opponents.

Players take turns drawing cards from a shared deck containing Safe Food, Poison Food, and Action Cards. While some cards allow players to survive safely, others can cost them valuable lives or give them opportunities to sabotage their opponents.

The objective is simple: survive until everyone else has been eliminated.

Game Information

  • Players: 3–8
  • Playing Time: 10–15 minutes
  • Game Type: Survival, Luck, Strategy, Party Game
  • Recommended Age: 13+
2. GAME OBJECTIVE

Be the last surviving player.

Every player starts with 3 lives. Drawing Poison Food causes players to lose lives, while Action Cards provide opportunities to heal, protect themselves, or create disadvantages for other players.

A player who loses all 3 lives is eliminated from the game.

The last player remaining wins.

3. GAME COMPONENTS

The game consists of a single shared deck divided into three categories.

A. SAFE FOOD CARDS

Safe Food Cards represent harmless food items.

  • Drawing a Safe Food Card has no negative or positive effect.
  • The player survives their turn.
  • The card is placed in the discard pile.
B. POISON FOOD CARDS

Poison Food Cards represent contaminated or poisonous food.

  • Drawing a Poison Food Card normally causes the player to lose 1 life.
  • If the player is under the effect of Double Damage, they lose 2 lives instead.
  • If the player has an active Immunity, they do not lose any lives.
C. ACTION CARDS

Action Cards introduce special abilities that can change the course of the game.

There are five types of Action Cards:

  1. Draw +1 Card
  2. Double Damage
  3. Heal Poison
  4. Immunity
  5. Reshuffle Deck
4. SETTING UP THE GAME
  1. Gather 3–8 players around a table.
  2. Combine all Safe Food, Poison Food, and Action Cards.
  3. Shuffle the entire deck thoroughly.
  4. Place the deck face down in the centre of the playing area.
  5. Each player starts with 3 lives.
  6. Keep a separate discard pile beside the main deck.
  7. Decide who goes first. Play proceeds clockwise.

Once everyone is ready, the game begins.

5. HOW TO PLAY

Players take turns drawing cards from the shared deck.

Step 1: Draw a Card

The active player picks one card from the top of the shuffled deck.

Step 2: Reveal the Card

The player reveals the card to everyone.

Step 3: Resolve the Card

The player follows the instructions according to the card drawn.

  • Safe Food: No effect. The player survives the turn.
  • Poison Food: The player loses 1 life, unless protected by Immunity or affected by Double Damage.
  • Action Card: The player performs the action described on the card.
Step 4: Discard the Card

After resolving its effect, the card is placed in the discard pile, unless it is an ongoing effect that must remain active.

Step 5: End the Turn

The turn passes to the next active player in clockwise order.

Players continue taking turns until only one player remains.

6. ACTION CARD RULES
1. DRAW +1 CARD

Effect: Draw one additional card immediately.

When a player draws this card:

  • They must immediately draw one additional card.
  • The additional card must be revealed.
  • Its effect must be resolved before the turn ends.
  • If the additional card is another Draw +1, the player must draw again.

Example: Player A draws Draw +1, followed by Poison Food. Player A loses 1 life.

2. DOUBLE DAMAGE

Effect: The next player takes double damage from Poison Food.

When a player draws this card:

  • The Double Damage effect is assigned to the next active player.
  • During that player's next turn, drawing Poison Food causes them to lose 2 lives instead of 1.
  • If the next player draws Safe Food or an Action Card, the Double Damage effect expires after that turn.
  • The effect cannot be transferred to another player.

Example: Player A draws Double Damage and passes the turn to Player B. Player B draws Poison Food and loses 2 lives.

3. HEAL POISON

Effect: Restore 1 lost life.

When a player draws this card:

  • They immediately recover 1 lost life.
  • A player cannot have more than 3 lives at any time.
  • If the player already has 3 lives, the card has no effect.
  • The card cannot revive an eliminated player.

Example: Player A has 1 remaining life. They draw Heal Poison and recover 1 life, bringing their total to 2.

4. IMMUNITY

Effect: Block the next Poison Food Card.

When a player draws this card:

  • They receive protection against their next Poison Food Card.
  • The next time they draw Poison Food, they lose no lives.
  • The Immunity is consumed immediately after blocking the Poison Food.
  • If they draw Safe Food or another Action Card, the Immunity remains active until used.
  • Immunity protects against both normal and Double Damage Poison attacks.

Example: Player A has Immunity and draws Poison Food while under Double Damage. The Poison Food is blocked, and Player A loses no lives. The Immunity is then discarded.

5. RESHUFFLE DECK

Effect: Reshuffle the available cards.

When a player draws this card:

  • Collect all cards from the discard pile and the remaining draw deck.
  • Combine them into one pile.
  • Shuffle thoroughly.
  • Place the newly shuffled deck in the centre of the playing area.
  • The next player continues the game.

All players retain their existing lives and active effects.

7. LIFE SYSTEM

Every player begins the game with 3 lives.

Each Poison Food Card normally removes 1 life.

Life progression:

  • 3 Lives: Completely healthy.
  • 2 Lives: Lost one life.
  • 1 Life: One final chance to survive.
  • 0 Lives: Eliminated.
Special Conditions
  • Double Damage removes 2 lives instead of 1.
  • Immunity prevents the loss of lives from the next Poison Food Card.
  • Heal Poison restores 1 lost life.
  • Maximum health is always 3 lives.
  • Players cannot go below 0 lives.
8. ELIMINATION RULES

A player is eliminated when their life count reaches zero.

Once eliminated:

  • They can no longer draw cards.
  • They cannot use Action Cards.
  • They cannot participate in future turns.
  • They are removed from the active playing order.

The game continues with the remaining players.

If a player loses their final life due to Double Damage, they are immediately eliminated.

9. WINNING THE GAME

The game continues until only one player has at least one remaining life.

That player is declared the winner of Food Your Way Out!

If multiple players are eliminated during the same card resolution, the game continues with the remaining active players.

10. IMPORTANT GAME RULES
  1. Every player starts with exactly 3 lives.
  2. All cards are drawn from one shared deck.
  3. Every drawn card must be revealed to all players.
  4. Card effects must be resolved immediately.
  5. Safe Food Cards have no effect.
  6. Poison Food Cards cause players to lose lives unless protected.
  7. Double Damage applies only to the next active player's next turn.
  8. Immunity blocks the next Poison Food Card, regardless of damage modifiers.
  9. Players cannot exceed the maximum of 3 lives.
  10. Eliminated players cannot return to the game.
  11. All resolved cards must be discarded.
  12. The game ends when only one player remains.
11. EXAMPLE GAMEPLAY ROUND

Imagine a game with four players: A, B, C and D.

No effect. | | B | Poison Food | Loses 1 life. | | C | Immunity | Gains protection. | | D | Double Damage | Targets A. | | A | Poison Food | Loses 2 lives. | | B | Heal Poison | Recovers 1 life. | | C | Poison Food | Immunity blocks it. |

The game continues with everyone tracking their remaining lives and active effects.
