How to play Blackjack

The goal: Beat the dealer: get closer to 21 than the dealer does, without going over.

Setup

You play against the dealer, not each other, and the table plays the dealer for you.

Your counter shows two numbers: your Chips (100 to start) and your Bet. Click a number to pick it, then use − and +.

The bar at the top runs the round: Bets, Players, Dealer, Payout. Press Next to move it on.

  1. Bets: everyone moves chips into their Bet. The table has gathered and shuffled the cards.
  2. Press Next. You get two cards in your hand: play them face up in front of you. The dealer gets one face up and one face down.

Card values

What cards are worth

  • 2 to 10: their number.
  • Jack, Queen, King: 10.
  • Ace: 1 or 11, whichever helps you.

An ace and a ten-card as your first two is blackjack, the best hand there is.

Your turn

Going round the table, each player in turn (the bar shows whose):

  • Hits: press Hit for another card, and play it face up. As often as you like.
  • Stands: keeps what they have.
  • Doubles down: doubles the bet, takes exactly one more card, then stands.

Go over 21 and you bust: you lose your bet straight away.

When you're done, press Next to pass to the next seat.

The dealer

The dealer plays last

After the last seat, Next brings the dealer's turn, and the table plays it: it turns over the hole card, then hits until the dealer has 17 or more, and stands. An ace counts 11 unless that would go over 21.

  • Dealer busts: everyone still in wins.
  • Otherwise, closer to 21 wins. A tie gets your bet back.

Press Next for Payout: a win gets your bet back plus the same again, blackjack pays 3 to 2. Next again starts a new round: the table gathers the cards and shuffles.
