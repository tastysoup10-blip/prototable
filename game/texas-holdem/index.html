How to play Texas Hold’em

The goal: Win the chips. Make the best five-card hand, or make everyone else fold.

Setup

  • Everyone starts with 100 chips: the counter in front of your seat.
  • The bar at the top runs the hand: Blinds, Pre-flop, Flop, Turn, River, Showdown. Press Next to move it on, and the table deals each part.
  • Put the D button in front of the dealer. The two players to the dealer's left put in the small blind (1) and the big blind (2): step your chips down and the Pot up.
  • Press Next. Everyone gets two cards in their hand. Only you can see yours.
  • Want a tournament? Press Start blinds clock: every ten minutes the table calls for the blinds to double.

A hand

How a hand goes

There are four rounds of betting. On your turn you can:

  • Fold: put your cards face down in the Muck. You're out of this hand.
  • Check: pass, if nobody has bet yet this round.
  • Call: match the biggest bet so far.
  • Raise: bet more. Everyone else then calls, raises again, or folds.

To bet, step your chips down and the Pot up by the same amount.

In each round the bar goes round the seats: when you've acted, press Next for the next player. After the last seat, Next deals the next round.

  1. Pre-flop: betting starts left of the big blind.
  2. Flop: three cards go face up in the middle. Bet.
  3. Turn: a fourth card. Bet.
  4. River: the fifth and last card. Bet.

If everyone else folds, the last player in takes the pot without showing.

Hand ranks

Best to worst

Make the best five cards from your two plus the five in the middle.

  1. Royal flush: A K Q J 10, one suit
  2. Straight flush: five in a row, one suit
  3. Four of a kind
  4. Full house: three of a kind plus a pair
  5. Flush: five of one suit
  6. Straight: five in a row
  7. Three of a kind
  8. Two pair
  9. One pair
  10. High card

Showdown

After the river's last seat, Next brings the Showdown. Everyone still in plays their two cards face up. The best hand takes the pot: step the Pot to 0 and add it to your chips. A tie splits it.

Move the D one seat left and press Next for the next hand. The table gathers every card back and shuffles.

Run out of chips and you're out. Last player with chips wins.
