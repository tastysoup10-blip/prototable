Eight letter-size pages of poker-size cards: a blank card with its corners marked, all 52 faces and a card back. Free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Good for: a custom deck, a gift, or a game that uses a standard deck. Size: Poker (64x89mm), one of the sizes built into the app.

How to lay it out

  • Rank and suit in two opposite corners, the second turned upside down.
  • Pips or a picture in the center, and space for the face cards’ art.
  • A symmetrical back, so a turned card gives nothing away.
  • Add two rows for jokers if your game uses them.

Use it in Prototable

  1. Press Use this template. This design and its 52 sample cards go straight into a game, with no account needed to start.

  3. Make a deck and put it on the table.

Questions

Is this playing card template free?

Yes. The printable PDF is free with no sign-up and no watermark. The sample deck is free to download and change, and Prototable is free for two games. Prints of your own cards from Prototable carry a small watermark on the free plan.

How do I print this template?

Download the PDF and print it at 100% or actual size, not fit to page, so the cards come out at 2.5 by 3.5 inches. Card stock holds up best. Cut along the outer lines.

Is this the real design?

Yes. The cards above are drawn from the same template you get, so it looks the same in Prototable, and you can change any part of it. Where the art goes, upload your own picture and it covers the placeholder.

How do I add my own cards?

Edit them in Prototable, or download the sample deck, change it in Excel or Google Sheets, and bring it in through Import, choosing this template.

Can I use these sample cards in my game?

Yes, they’re yours to change or use, though you’ll want to replace them with your own.

Do I need an account?

Not to start: you can open a table and import cards without one. Sign up when you want to keep your work.
