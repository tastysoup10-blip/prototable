Get your game on the table in 4 steps

Prototable is a table in your browser for testing a tabletop game while it is still changing. Here is how to go from nothing to your first recorded playtest.

Before you start

You don't need an account to start, and there's nothing to install. Sign up when you want to keep your game. Players you invite never need an account or to pay: they join from a link in their browser, on a laptop, desktop, iPad or phone.

1/ Bring in your cards

Press New game at the top of the sidebar. It asks where your cards are now (signed out, press Create your own game on prototable.gg, and Build your game asks the same at your table):

From Tabletop SimulatorBring in your deck's card sheet, the image with every card on it. Each card is cut out, ready to play.
From a spreadsheetOne row per card. Upload a CSV or paste from Google Sheets or Excel, and your columns become card fields.
From scratchStart a card and write it. Every card after it follows the same layout.

A spreadsheet is the quickest way to change many cards later. Put one card on each row and one field in each column:

cards.csv
Name,Cost,Type,Text
Ember Fox,2,Creature,Gain 1 spark.
Tidecaller,3,Creature,Draw a card.
Old Oak,5,Creature,Block twice.
Night Owl,1,Creature,Peek at the deck.

Already drew your cards as images? Choose card images. Want to see the table first? Explore has poker, blackjack, Hearts, chess, checkers and Crazy Eights to play. Every way in, step by step: Adding cards.

A new game opens on its Overview: a checklist from your first card to playing it with a friend, each step ticking itself off as you go.

2/ Lay them out and set the table

On your game's Cards page, Edit template places your fields (name, cost, type, text, art) on the card once, with your own fonts, colors, icons, border and back. Every card made from that template follows it, so moving the cost to the other corner moves it on every card. More in Card templates.

Make a deck in Decks: a list of cards and how many of each. To lay out the table, press Edit at the table (Shift E) and add piles, lanes, mats, dice, tokens and counters from Add. Write your rules on the Rules page; players read them in the rulebook at the table.

Try it alone first. Choose Set up stand-ins to fill the other seats and play every one yourself, switching between them. You'll find the rules that don't work before anyone else does.

3/ Invite players and press Record

Sign in, then press Share (at the top of every page, and at the table), choose who can join, and press Copy the invite link:

  • Open: anyone with the link walks straight in.
  • PIN: they need the PIN as well as the link.
  • Approval: they knock, and you let them in.

Each player gets a seat and a hand for hidden cards. Press Record when the game starts.

When a card is clearly wrong, edit it mid-game: every copy updates at once, on the table, in hands and in decks. Right-click a card and choose Add note to write down what happened. More in Playtesting with others.

4/ Review, then play again

Open your game's Playtests. Every card edited during a recorded playtest waits under Card changes: Keep makes the change stick, Discard puts the card back. Read what players said, then play the next version. More in Recording and review.

Everything about a game is one row of tabs under its name, and Make, the first button at your table's top left (or press M), holds the same things, with the table still in view beside it:

TabFor
OverviewYour checklist, this week's changes and your plan
CardsEvery card, its template, importing, printing and ideas
PiecesBoards, tokens and dice
DecksWhich cards, how many of each, and deck lists from TCGs
RulesHow it's played, and the turn's steps
PlaytestsRecordings, card changes and what players said

Publish, beside Open table, sends the game out. T takes you back to your table from any page, G lists your games, and H opens Explore.

Questions

Do I need an account to use Prototable?

No. You can start your game without one and sign up when you want to keep it. Players you invite never need an account.

Do my players have to pay or install anything?

No. Players join free from a link in their browser, on a laptop, desktop, iPad or phone.

Is Prototable free?

Yes, for two games, with 2 players a room. Designer ($59 a year) adds unlimited games, 6 players a room and unlimited recorded playtests.

Can I bring in my Tabletop Simulator deck?

Yes. Choose a Tabletop Simulator deck in New game or Import, and each card on its card sheet is cut out, ready to play and to print.
